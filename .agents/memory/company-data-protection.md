name: Company-data protection
description: Owner's distinction between valid company information and operational/demo data.
---

Key Personnel & Contacts are valid company information. Do not delete, reset, replace, or modify Key Personnel names, contact details, project groups, or records as part of operational-data cleanup.

Preserve Admin accounts, user permissions, project lists, departments, and asset classifications. SOS remains Operator direct-close without Admin approval.

**Why:** The owner explicitly identified these as valid company data and required them to be protected while correcting operational dashboard/report data.

**How to apply:** Do not treat company information as demo fixtures merely because it originated in seed code. Keep cleanup and accuracy corrections scoped to the operational data concerned.
